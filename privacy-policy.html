Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Stiller Sealcoating ("we", "us", or "our") operates stillersealcoating.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your choices. It applies only to this website.

1. Who we are

Stiller Sealcoating provides driveway sealcoating, crack filling and repair, commercial asphalt maintenance, and HOA and multi-family lot maintenance in Mooresville, Camby, Monrovia, Martinsville, Morgantown, Centerton, Plainfield, Avon, and Brownsburg, Indiana, and the surrounding west Indianapolis metro. Our location is Mooresville, Indiana.

2. Information we collect

When you use the contact or request form on this website, we collect your name, email address, phone number, service address, and photos or details you share about your driveway or lot. We collect this information only when you choose to provide it.

We also collect basic technical information automatically when you visit the site, such as the pages you visit, the type of device and browser you use, and general location information based on your internet address. We do not use this information to identify you by name unless you contact us.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your request, prepare a quote or estimate, schedule or provide service, and follow up with you about your inquiry. We may also use it to improve this website and our services.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run this website and respond to you, for example with our form provider (Netlify Forms), our email provider, and our website host. We do not share your information for marketing by other companies.

We may share information if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property, or the safety of others.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website, such as which pages are visited and how visitors found the site. Google Analytics uses cookies for this purpose. You can opt out with your browser settings or a browser add-on from Google.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep inquiry and service information only as long as needed to respond to you, provide service, and keep business records. You can ask us to delete your inquiry information by contacting us, unless we need to keep it for legal or accounting reasons.

7. Your choices

You can choose not to provide personal information through the form, and contact us by phone or email instead where those options are listed. You can ask us what information we have about you from a website inquiry, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us below.

8. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website. If you believe a child has provided information through this site, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Continued use of the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.
